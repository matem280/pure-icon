# Contributing to Pure Icons

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Pure Icons.

Pure Icons is an icon design project created by HASSEL. Contributions should preserve the visual consistency, geometric structure, and material language defined in the official design guide.

## Before Contributing

Before creating or submitting an icon, please review the official design guide in the `design-guide/` directory.

Every contribution should follow the project's principles of consistency, clarity, craftsmanship, and accessibility.

## Contribution Requirements

Submitted icons should:

- Follow the geometric system and proportions defined in the design guide.
- Use the established layer architecture, colours, gradients, and lighting effects.
- Remain recognizable at their intended display sizes.
- Maintain visual consistency with the existing icon collection.
- Be technically suitable for use in digital interfaces.

## File Formats

SVG is the preferred format for submitted icons.

Where appropriate, include the editable source file used to create the icon.

Please use clear, descriptive, lowercase filenames. Separate words with hyphens where necessary.

Examples:

- `calendar.svg`
- `music-player.svg`
- `file-manager.svg`

## Submitting a Contribution

1. Review the official design guide.
2. Prepare the icon and check its visual and technical quality.
3. Fork the repository.
4. Add your proposed icon to your fork.
5. Open a pull request describing the icon and any relevant design decisions.
6. Respond to feedback during the review process.

## Review Process

All submissions are subject to review.

A contribution may be accepted, returned for revision, or declined if it does not meet the project's design or technical requirements.

Submitting an icon does not guarantee its inclusion in the official collection.

Only reviewed and approved icons will be merged into the official `icons/` directory.

## Licensing

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit the work and that it does not knowingly infringe the rights of others.

Contributors must agree to the licensing terms applicable to accepted contributions. Review the repository's `LICENSE` file before submitting work.

## Questions

If you have questions about the design system or contribution process, open an issue in the repository.

Thank you for helping develop Pure Icons.
